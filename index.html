01 / THE STORY

A small team with an unusually big appetite for making things. Music, film, photography, technology and design all live under one roof.

Started with nothing.
Built with everything.

DCRS Productions began with four Class 12 students in Thrissur, Kerala who wanted a place where their talents could collide. Instead of waiting for the “right time,” they started now.

One writes and sings. One builds wild ideas with foam board and IoT. One turns moments into songs. One frames the story through cameras and edits. Different strengths. One team.
